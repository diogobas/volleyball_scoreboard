Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy explains the data practices of Volleyball Scoreboard, developed and operated by Diogo Villa Verde Bastos, including the mobile scoreboard and its companion group-results and administration website.

You can score independently offline without an operator account. Loading a game-day lineup or syncing a result uses the online service. Synced player names and match statistics can be publicly visible on the companion website.

1. Data we use and why

  • Local match data: team and player names, points, set scores, scoring history, substitutions, match identifiers, dates, and saved matches are stored on your device to provide the scoreboard, statistics and offline recovery. Names can be nicknames. Independent play does not automatically upload your match data.
  • Game-day access: when you submit a game-day code, the service receives that code and returns the group, date and prepared lineup. The app stores the linked game-day context so that you can play offline and later sync the result.
  • Synced results: choosing Save & sync, or manually linking and syncing a saved match, sends its group/day association, team and participant names or identifiers, scores, player points, recorded scoring/lineup history and match timestamps to the server. This supports durable storage, duplicate prevention and group statistics. A normal Save keeps the result on the device until you choose to sync.
  • Group administration: authorized website administrators can register player names and Fixed/Sub categories, manage game days, and create or correct match records. The website records administrative actions and reasons for accountability. Historical records may contain only set scores or optional whole-match player totals.
  • Optional administrator accounts: the companion website uses email addresses and authentication data to create and authenticate administrator accounts and manage group access. Authentication is handled by Supabase. The mobile scoreboard does not require an operator account.
  • Technical requests: when using online features or visiting the website, infrastructure providers may process IP addresses, browser/device information and request logs to deliver, protect and troubleshoot the service.
  • Messages to us: if you contact us, we use your email address, message and information you supply to respond and handle your request.

2. Public results and disclosure

Group names, player display names, Fixed/Sub categories, match dates, team names, set results and individual points, attendance and wins may be accessible without signing in on the companion website. People who receive a game-day code can retrieve that day's lineup. Administrator email addresses, authentication data, private submissions and administrative audit records are not part of the public-results interface.

Group administrators and operators should enter and publish only information they are authorized to use, and inform participants that results are public. Contact the group administrator or the developer if a name or result should be corrected or removed.

3. Providers, sharing and security

We use Supabase for the database and administrator authentication, and Cloudflare for the main results website. These providers process information needed to operate those services. Email sent to our contact address is handled by Google Gmail. App-store providers also process distribution and testing information under their own policies. Their privacy information is available at Supabase Privacy Policy and Cloudflare Privacy Policy. Online service data may be processed outside your country.

Online connections use HTTPS. Administrator operations require authorized access; public results are deliberately readable by anyone. No system can guarantee absolute security. Device security and operating-system backup settings also affect local information.

We do not sell personal data, serve advertising or integrate advertising identifiers or third-party behavioral analytics SDKs in this version. The app does not request access to contacts, precise location, camera or microphone. Website sign-in uses local browser storage to maintain your administrator session; match storage is used for offline operation, not advertising.

4. Retention, correction and deletion

On your device: active and saved match data remain until you reset the active match, delete a saved match, clear app/site storage or uninstall the app, subject to device backups. Resetting the active match does not delete previously saved matches. Deleting an unsynced saved match prevents its future upload. After a successful sync, the app removes the saved local copy only after server confirmation. Removing a local copy does not delete a result already synced to the server.

On the server: synced results and registrations do not have an automatic expiry. They are retained to provide match history and season statistics until corrected, removed, anonymized or erased as appropriate. Administrative removal can hide a match or player from public results while original submissions, correction history and deletion/audit records remain to preserve integrity and prevent deleted matches from being restored by retries.

Privacy and account requests: email the contact below to request access, correction, erasure of personal information, or deletion of an administrator website account and its associated personal data. Identify the group, player name or account email and, if relevant, the match/date. We may ask for information needed to verify the request. We will explain any information that must be retained for security, integrity or applicable legal obligations, and remove or anonymize other relevant personal information as appropriate. A request is not completed merely by disabling an account or hiding a result.

Infrastructure backup and technical-log retention depends on the provider and service configuration; we do not promise immediate deletion from all backup copies. Contact us for questions about a specific request.

5. Children and policy changes

The service is a volleyball scoring tool and is not specifically directed at children. Administrators should avoid publishing information about children without appropriate permission. If you believe a child's personal data has been entered improperly, contact us to request review and removal.

We may update this policy when data practices or features change. The date above identifies the current version.

Você pode apagar partidas locais salvas e reiniciar a partida atual. Isso não apaga resultados já sincronizados. Os registros no servidor não expiram automaticamente; a remoção administrativa pode ocultar um resultado e preservar o histórico privado para auditoria. Para solicitar acesso, correção, exclusão de dados pessoais ou exclusão de uma conta administrativa e dos seus dados associados, entre em contato por e-mail e indique o grupo/jogador/partida ou a conta envolvida. Podemos verificar a identidade do solicitante e explicar eventuais retenções necessárias. Backups e registros técnicos seguem a configuração dos provedores.

O serviço não é especificamente destinado a crianças. Administradores devem ter a autorização apropriada para registrar e publicar os dados dos participantes. Alterações nesta política serão indicadas pela data no início da página.
